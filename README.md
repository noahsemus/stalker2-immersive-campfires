# Immersive Campfires

A mod for **S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl** that lets you stay seated at the campfire: eat, drink, heal,
open the PDA and backpack and play the guitar without getting up, with the full first-person animations.

Vanilla STALKER 2 locks the campfire sit to a handful of keys: no items, no PDA, no backpack. This mod keeps Skif
seated and lets the game do all of it.

**Pure pak mod, no UE4SS.** Built with the official S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 Zone Kit.

## Features

- **Use items while seated:** food, drinks, medkits, bandages, injectors and other consumables, from the quick
  slots, the item wheel or the backpack, with their full vanilla animations. Items from other mods (Project
  Itemization Reborn and others) work too.
- **PDA and backpack** open while seated.
- **Free look.** The body and legs stay planted on the seat while you look around; the camera is always yours.
- **Natural arms.** After an item, the arms ease straight into the lap; the game's own "reach for the weapon" at the
  end of every item and its weapon re-draw are kept out of view.
- **Guitar** as in vanilla (G); put it away and you are seated again. The "Play the guitar" prompt no longer pops up.
- **Clean stand-up.** Any movement key, jump, interact or Esc stands you up; no sliding, the body does not turn and
  the view settles level and forward. Your weapon comes back.
- **Your own key bindings** from Options > Controls work while seated.
- **Sleeping Bag Mod compatible:** use the sleeping bag while seated to stand up and get its sleep window.

## Requirements

STALKER 2 (Steam or Epic), current patch. Nothing else.

## Install

Vortex: drop the zip in, install, deploy. Manual: copy the six files into `<game>\Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\`
(create `~mods` if needed). Uninstall: delete them; nothing is stored in your save.

## Compatibility

Overrides two game assets: `IMC_PlayerCA` (the seated controls) and `HUDContextualLegend` (the bottom key-hint
line, hidden; it also carried the drop-body and dialogue-skip hints). A mod replacing either one conflicts (the last
loaded wins). No config files. Works alongside [Immersive Dialogue](https://github.com/noahsemus/stalker2-immersive-dialogue).

Everything else runs in a Blueprint of the mod's own (a world subsystem spawning one actor): no other game asset is
replaced, so animation, weapon and HUD mods are unaffected.
